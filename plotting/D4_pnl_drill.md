**Data**: run `python3 make_data.py` once to create these files.
- `data/d4_pnl_daily.parquet`: `date`, `strategy`, `pnl` ($ per day), covering 120 trading days.
- `data/d4_pnl_by_symbol.parquet`: S1's total PnL per `symbol` over the same period.

For each question: choose a chart and say **why that chart**, make it, then explain what it shows in 2–3 sentences.

1. How did each strategy perform over the period?
2. How risky is S3?
3. Where does S1's PnL come from?
4. Do the four strategies diversify each other?
5. Did the S4 deployment hurt performance? How confident are you?

**Present**: give the four-sentence summary you'd open the review with.
